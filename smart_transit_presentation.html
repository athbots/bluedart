• Edge Computer Vision & Temporal Hysteresis
Zero-Cloud Video Transmission
Raw video never leaves the bus. Processing runs locally on Raspberry Pi 4 / Jetson Nano, guaranteeing commuter privacy under DPDP regulations.
Contour Area Density Metric
Cabin frames undergo Gaussian blur, background segmentation, and contour bounding box clustering to compute passenger occupancy ratio.
3-Stage Temporal Anti-Flicker Filter
O_t = mode(c_{t-2}, c_{t-1}, c_t)
Transitions between LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, VERY_HIGH require multi-frame hysteresis confirmation.
Density Classification Thresholds
LOW: 0-35% (all seated) | MEDIUM: 36-70% (light standing) | HIGH: 71-95% (crowded) | VERY HIGH: >95% (crush load)
• Offline-First SQLite Buffer Architecture
Cellular Blackspot Dilemma
Highways and underpasses trigger frequent 4G/5G signal loss. Conventional systems lose state, creating phantom radar stalls.
Edge SQLite WAL Ring Buffer
During connectivity drops, the onboard daemon writes every GPS coordinate and crowd estimate to an offline SQLite database with Write-Ahead Logging.
Seamless Atomic Replay Synchronization
Upon cellular reconnect, batches are pushed atomically to /api/v1/devices/buffer-sync with dual timestamps: device_timestamp and server_received_timestamp.
Historical Audit Protection
The backend preserves replayed pings for ML training but prevents stale coordinates from overwriting current live tracking blips.

• Active Corridor Load Balancing
The "Bunched Bus" Paradox in Indian Transit
Two buses on the same route arrive 4 minutes apart. The first is dangerously overcrowded (95%+), while the trailing bus is nearly empty (30%). Commuters mob the first, exacerbating route delays.
FASTEST Arrival Recommendation
Assigned to the vehicle arriving first at the commuter's stop with acceptable boarding capacity.
Target Commuter: Time-sensitive passengers and rush-hour commuters.
MOST COMFORTABLE Recommendation
Identifies a trailing bus arriving within a threshold window (Δt ≤ 10 min) with LOW or MEDIUM crowd occupancy.
Target Commuter: Elderly, families, and long-distance riders desiring seats.
Empirical Corridor Benefit
Balances boarding queues and reduces station dwell time congestion by 18-24%.
